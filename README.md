# Retail Sales Performance Dashboard

A BI project analyzing 9,994 rows of retail sales data to identify profitability problems and provide actionable recommendations.

**Live Dashboard:** https://public.tableau.com/views/RetailSalesDashboard_17807868488810/Dashboard1

**Tools:** Excel, Tableau Public

---

## Business Problem

The company generates $2.3M in revenue but only 12.47% profit margin. The goal was to find out why and recommend fixes.

---

## Key Findings

1. Furniture Tables lose $17,725 despite $206K in sales — caused by 26% average discounting
2. Technology (Copiers, Phones, Accessories) generates $142K profit at only 8-16% discount
3. West region leads with 14.9% margin. Central region underperforms at 7.9% margin
4. Consumer segment drives 51% of total revenue ($1.16M)

---

## Recommendations

- Cap discounts at 20% across all products — estimated $21K annual profit recovery
- Shift marketing budget toward Technology category
- Investigate Central region discounting practices
